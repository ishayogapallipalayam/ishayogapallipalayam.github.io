Isha Yoga program (Inner Engineering) with Shambhavi Mahamudra Kriya

The Isha Yoga program is called Inner Engineering in English. Over 7 days it offers tools for wellbeing and initiation into Shambhavi Mahamudra Kriya, a powerful 21-minute meditation practice. Once initiated, you can practise Shambhavi at home every day.

The program is held in Tamil as the Isha Yoga program and in English as Inner Engineering. It is open to anyone who can sit for the session timings; no prior yoga experience is needed.

Free Surya Kriya yoga class

Surya Kriya is a classical hatha yoga practice offered by Isha. Free Surya Kriya classes are held from time to time in Pallipalayam and are suitable for beginners. Call 7010692011 or WhatsApp us to know the next class date.

Devi Pooja, satsang and special days

We also hold Devi Pooja, satsangs and special days such as Mahashivratri, Guru Purnima and International Yoga Day for the local community. Announcements are posted on our Google Maps listing.
