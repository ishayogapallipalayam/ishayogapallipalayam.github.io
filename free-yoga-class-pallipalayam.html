Free yoga class: questions

Is there a free yoga class in Pallipalayam?

Yes. A free yoga class (Surya Shakti) is on Sunday, 15 November 2026, 7:00 to 9:00 am, at the Isha Yoga Pallipalayam centre. Registration is free at isha.co/ss-pallipalayam.

Is the class really free?

Yes. The class is offered free of cost by Isha volunteers. There is no fee to register or attend.

Can beginners join?

Yes. No yoga experience is needed. Students, working professionals, homemakers and senior citizens can join.

Is there a free yoga class near Erode?

Pallipalayam is just across the Cauvery from Erode, about 5 km from Erode bus stand, so people from Erode, Kumarapalayam, Veppadai and Bhavani attend.

How do I register?

Register online at isha.co/ss-pallipalayam, or call 7010692011.
