Isha yoga Erode: classes for people in Erode city

Isha Yoga Program (Inner Engineering)

The Isha Yoga program, known as Inner Engineering in English, is designed by Sadhguru. It includes initiation into Shambhavi Mahamudra Kriya, a 21-minute practice you can continue at home every day. Held in Tamil and in English at Pallipalayam.

Surya Kriya Yoga Class
