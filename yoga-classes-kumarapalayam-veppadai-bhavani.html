Programs you can join

7 days

Isha Yoga Program (Inner Engineering)

The Isha Yoga program, known as Inner Engineering in English, is designed by Sadhguru. It includes initiation into Shambhavi Mahamudra Kriya, a 21-minute practice you can continue at home every day. Held in Tamil and in English at Pallipalayam.

Free

Surya Kriya Yoga Class

Surya Kriya is a classical hatha yoga practice from Isha. Free Surya Kriya classes are held from time to time at Pallipalayam and are open to beginners. Free class details.

Events

Devi Pooja and special events

Devi Pooja, satsangs and special days such as Mahashivratri, Guru Purnima and International Yoga Day are held for the local community. Follow our Google Maps listing for announcements.
