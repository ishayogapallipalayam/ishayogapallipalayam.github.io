Isha yoga programs in Pallipalayam

Isha Yoga Pallipalayam is run by local Isha volunteers. We conduct the Isha Yoga program (Inner Engineering), free Surya Kriya yoga classes and community events, so that people from Pallipalayam and Erode can learn Isha practices close to home.

7 days

Isha Yoga Program (Inner Engineering)

The Isha Yoga program, known as Inner Engineering in English, is designed by Sadhguru. It includes initiation into Shambhavi Mahamudra Kriya, a 21-minute practice you can continue at home every day. Held in Tamil and in English at Pallipalayam.

Free

Surya Kriya Yoga Class

Surya Kriya is a classical hatha yoga practice from Isha. Free Surya Kriya classes are held from time to time at Pallipalayam and are open to beginners. Call or WhatsApp to know the next date.

Events

Devi Pooja and special events

Devi Pooja, satsangs and special days such as Mahashivratri, Guru Purnima and International Yoga Day are held for the local community. Follow our Google Maps listing for announcements.

Why people join Isha yoga classes

Simple daily practice

Shambhavi Mahamudra Kriya takes about 21 minutes a day and can be done at home once you are initiated.

Better sleep and calm

Studies of Shambhavi practitioners, including research involving Harvard Medical School, report improvements in sleep quality and wellbeing.

For everyone

Students, working professionals, homemakers and senior citizens join the same program. No prior yoga experience is needed.

Yoga classes for Erode, Kumarapalayam and nearby areas

Pallipalayam is just across the Cauvery from Erode, about 5 km from Erode bus stand. People join our programs from Erode city areas such as Karungalpalayam, Surampatti and Veerappanchatram, and from Kumarapalayam, Veppadai, Bhavani, Tiruchengode and Sankagiri.

Frequently asked questions

Where are Isha yoga classes held in Pallipalayam?

The current 7-day Isha Yoga program is held at Kongu Kalaiarangam, Vediyarasampalayam, Pallipalayam, just across the Cauvery from Erode. Venues can change for each program, so please check the program details or call before you come.

Is there a free yoga class?

Yes. Free Surya Kriya yoga classes are held from time to time. Call or WhatsApp us to know the next date.

Do I need any yoga experience?

No. The programs are open to complete beginners, students, working professionals, women and senior citizens.

In which language are the classes held?

The Isha Yoga program is held in Tamil, and Inner Engineering is held in English.

How do I register?

Register online at isha.co/pallipalayam, or call 7010692011 or 9842773480.
